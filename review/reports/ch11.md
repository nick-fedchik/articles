## Findings and Corrections

**R11-01 — Project risk parameter presented as a standard criterion.** Evidence: the original §10 called `τ_risk = 0.001` a “нормативний критерій” for ASIL D/SIL 3 and applied it to general `R̂(r)` ([current corrected passage](../../ExpertSystem/ch11-knowledge-elicitation-from-experts.md#L488)); the registry separately says statistical scoring does not replace the evidence basis ([CH11-S06-08](../../ExpertSystem/registry/ch11-registry.md#L518)). After: §10 and [CH11-S10-04](../../ExpertSystem/registry/ch11-registry.md#L729) identify 0.001 as an author-proposed project parameter for binary failure frequency, distinguish it from general scaled loss, and restrict `n = 2,995` to the independent-binomial/zero-failure assumptions. Updated [REQ-L1-11-10](../../ExpertSystem/requirements/L1-stakeholder/ch11-L1-stakeholder.md#L98), [REQ-L2-11-23](../../ExpertSystem/requirements/L2-system/ch11-L2-system.md#L215), and [REQ-L3-11-37](../../ExpertSystem/requirements/L3-component/ch11-L3-component.md#L341). **Reader consequence:** the example no longer implies an ASIL threshold or certification; assumptions and the remaining evidence gap are visible.

**R11-02 — Asymptotic complexity represented as WCET.** Evidence: the original §14 asserted “гарантованою часовою межею `$O(1)$`” and a “юридично неспростовний аудиторський слід” tied to ISO 26262/IEC 61508 ([current corrected passage](../../ExpertSystem/ch11-knowledge-elicitation-from-experts.md#L632)); [CH11-S14-06](../../ExpertSystem/registry/ch11-registry.md#L921) repeated it. After: both now require a separate time bound for a stated hardware/input profile and say `O(1)` alone does not establish WCET. Updated [REQ-L1-11-14](../../ExpertSystem/requirements/L1-stakeholder/ch11-L1-stakeholder.md#L134), [REQ-L2-11-31](../../ExpertSystem/requirements/L2-system/ch11-L2-system.md#L287), and [REQ-L3-11-46](../../ExpertSystem/requirements/L3-component/ch11-L3-component.md#L422). **Reader consequence:** a complexity class is no longer mistaken for measured or proven execution time, and an audit log is not promised an uncontestable legal effect.

**R11-03 — Storage/deletion architecture presented as GDPR compatibility.** Evidence: the original §4 said the design coordinated with GDPR deletion requirements ([current corrected passage](../../ExpertSystem/ch11-knowledge-elicitation-from-experts.md#L206)); §9 claimed compatibility with Art. 17 and physical deletion on request ([current corrected passage](../../ExpertSystem/ch11-knowledge-elicitation-from-experts.md#L460)). Registry entries [CH11-S04-05](../../ExpertSystem/registry/ch11-registry.md#L374) and [CH11-S09-04](../../ExpertSystem/registry/ch11-registry.md#L681) repeated the compliance framing. After: these passages present a design option requiring case-specific legal assessment. Updated [REQ-L1-11-12](../../ExpertSystem/requirements/L1-stakeholder/ch11-L1-stakeholder.md#L116) and [REQ-L2-11-28](../../ExpertSystem/requirements/L2-system/ch11-L2-system.md#L260). **Reader consequence:** the architecture is not mistaken for a legal determination; metadata, retention, and deletion grounds remain open for review.

**R11-04 — VOI stopping inequality has an unbound question variable.** Evidence: §7 combined a maximum over `q` on the benefit with a question-specific cost (`C_expert(q) + C_validation(q)`) ([current corrected passage](../../ExpertSystem/ch11-knowledge-elicitation-from-experts.md#L372)); [CH11-S07-06](../../ExpertSystem/registry/ch11-registry.md#L587) copied it. After: both compare each admissible question’s expected loss reduction with that same question’s costs, and stop only when the condition holds for every candidate. **Reader consequence:** the stopping rule is operationally interpretable without changing its existing cost or risk assumptions.

No new empirical values, sources, thresholds, or certification claims were introduced. The existing 0.001 and 2,995 values remain explicitly conditional/project-defined, not calibrated evidence. External source verification and empirical validation were not performed.
