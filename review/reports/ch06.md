## R06-01: Farkas Witness Is Not A Minimal Repair

Priority P1. Confirmed and corrected locally. Locations: chapter 9.3 authorial
NOTE item 1, 9.3.1, runtime action, numerical example, and Dashboard; registry
CH06-S09-05; REQ-L3-06-34. Authorial synthesis, names, and attribution retained.

Exact before excerpt:

```text
Ненульові індекси $`y_i > 0`$ детерміновано виокремлюють мінімальну підсистему несумісних обмежень (Minimal Unsatisfiable Subsystem, MUS).
```

Exact after excerpt:

```text
Ненульові індекси $`y_i > 0`$ виокремлюють несумісну підсистему обмежень; її мінімальність потребує окремої перевірки, перш ніж називати її мінімальною несумісною підсистемою (Minimal Unsatisfiable Subsystem, MUS).
```

The original runtime also equated the infeasibility witness with optimal
sensitivity, using this exact expression:

```text
$`\partial \Phi / \partial b_i = y_i^*`$
```

That expression was removed. Exact replacement sentence:

```text
Мінімальне послаблення обчислюють окремою задачею з явно заданими дозволеними змінами та мірою їхньої вартості.
```

Evidence: the chapter's witness gives A-transpose-y = (0,150) and b-transpose-y
= -40. Doubling it gives (0,300) and -80 while certifying the same infeasible
system. A separate exact counterexample has A = [(1),(-1),(1)], b = (0,-1,2),
y = (2,3,1): all three supported constraints certify infeasibility, but the
first two already conflict. Thus support need not be minimal. This is a
synthetic arithmetic counterexample, not an experiment or a new book theorem.

The existing continuous numerical model was made dimensionally explicit:
nonnegative dimensionless real design variables, mass coefficients in kg, power
coefficients in W. The original 0.134 kg recommendation is sufficient, but not
the exact minimum: 400/300 - 1.2 = 2/15 kg. The text now distinguishes this
minimum from rounding the mass limit upward to 1.334 kg. It retains the valid
Farkas certificate and `INFEASIBLE_CERTIFIED` status, and does not present an
approved change of requirements as fulfillment of the original requirements.

Reader consequence: an infeasibility diagnosis cannot silently become an
authorization to relax a safety requirement or a claim that a minimum repair
has been computed. Open author-policy choice: permitted changes and their cost
metric must be specified before a general minimum-repair optimization; this
review did not choose such a policy or convert the model into an integer BoM.

## R06-02: Pipeline Period Is Not Query WCET

Priority P1. Confirmed and corrected locally. Locations: chapter 9.3 authorial
NOTE item 4, 9.3.3, sizing action, and Under-the-Hood example; registry
CH06-S09-07/08; REQ-L2-06-27 and REQ-L3-06-36.

Exact before excerpt:

```text
> 4. **Тропічна (Max-Plus) лінійна алгебра конвеєрів:** Детермінований розрахунок найгіршого часу виконання (`WCET`) апаратних прискорювачів знань (EPU) через головне власне число матриці переходів.
```

Exact after excerpt:

```text
> 4. **Тропічна (Max-Plus) лінійна алгебра конвеєрів:** Розрахунок асимптотичного періоду видачі порцій апаратного прискорювача знань (EPU) через максимальне середнє циклу; найгірший час виконання (`WCET`) конкретного запиту потребує окремого аналізу скінченного виконання.
```

Exact old sizing expression:

```text
$`V_{\mathrm{chunk}} \le B_{\mathrm{bus}} \cdot \lambda_{\max}`$
```

Exact replacement expressions:

```text
$`T_{\mathrm{refill}} \le T_{\mathrm{execute}}`$
$`V_{\mathrm{chunk}} \le B_{\mathrm{bus}} \cdot T_{\mathrm{execute}}`$
```

The replacement explicitly limits the bandwidth product to constant guaranteed
bandwidth without DMA overhead; the actual bank schedule and worst refill
time must be checked. The trace formula was retained in meaning, rewritten
using ordinary division by cycle length, and its finite strongly connected
fixed-delay model, time units, and symbols were defined.

Evidence: synthetic Max-Plus A = [(18,0),(36,18)] with initial times (0,0)
has maximum cycle mean 18 but completion vectors (18,36), then (36,54).
Period 18 does not bound first completion 36. Another exact control transfers
18 bytes at 1 byte/ns into an 18 ns window: ideal transfer fits; adding 1 ns
DMA startup makes it miss. These are counterexample fixtures, not measured
pipeline results. The chapter's existing 18 ns figure is now explicitly
educational asymptotic period, not measured or proven WCET.

Reader consequence: buffer sizing and deadline claims must use the actual
execution window and transition/resource costs. Residual status: target
hardware WCET and a complete DMA schedule remain unverified; no hardware
timing bound was invented or certified.

```text
- $`d_M`$ є відстанню Махаланобіса, невід'ємним безрозмірним числом, а $`d_M^2`$ за узгодженої моделі має розподіл хі-квадрат із $m$ ступенями вільності [[42]](#src-42), [[43]](#src-43).
```

Exact after excerpt:

```text
- $`d_M`$ є відстанню Махаланобіса, невід'ємним безрозмірним числом. Точний закон $`d_M^2 \sim \chi_m^2`$ виконується для центрованої гауссівської інновації $`\tilde{y} \sim \mathcal{N}(0,S)`$ з відомою додатно визначеною коваріацією $S \succ 0$ [[42]](#src-42), [[43]](#src-43).
```

Evidence: each of two independent synthetic coordinates is zero with mass
24/25 and +5/-5 with mass 1/50 each. Mean is exactly zero and covariance
exactly the identity, yet the 9.21 squared-distance gate accepts only
576/625 = 92.16%, not 99%. The Gaussian two-dimensional positive control uses
the exact CDF 1-exp(-q/2); q = 9.21 gives approximately 99%. Singular covariance
and the unchanged original diagonal example are separately checked.

The text also states the uncorrelated prediction-error/measurement-noise
assumption needed for S = H P-minus H-transpose + R, the distinction between
known and estimated covariance, and the undefined inverse for singular S.
The 3.03 distance is identified as an approximation to sqrt(9.21), not a new
exact threshold. Existing example accept/reject outcomes were preserved.

Reader consequence: consistent second moments alone no longer justify a 99%
acceptance claim, and passing the gate is not proof of physical truth.
Residual status: field noise distribution, estimated-covariance calibration,
and the approved fallback for a degenerate model remain application-specific
and unverified here; no empirical calibration was invented.
