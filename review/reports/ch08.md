## R08-01: Conditional Cascade Probabilities

**Priority / status:** P1, confirmed local model ambiguity; safe mathematical correction completed. Locations: chapter section 2, initial lines 37-45 / final lines 37-47; registry `CH08-S02-01`; `REQ-L3-08-03`.

The product of marginal stage accuracies is not generally the probability that every stage succeeds. The existing authorial name was retained, while the underlying classical chain rule and its assumptions were made explicit. The model describes a chain requiring every stage to succeed, not a chain that repairs earlier mistakes.

Before, the parameter definition immediately below the product was:

```text
де $`\varepsilon_i \in [0, 1)`$ — похибка $i$-го кроку інформаційного вилучення, а $k$ — глибина ланцюга обробки:
```

After, the exact defining sentence in that position is:

```text
Для ненульового правильного префікса $`\varepsilon_i=P(\neg A_i\mid A_1\cap\dots\cap A_{i-1})\in[0,1]`$ є умовною ймовірністю помилки, а для першого етапу $`\varepsilon_1=P(\neg A_1)`$.
```

Counterexample, not an empirical dataset: in a population of 100, three stages fail on disjoint groups of 5, 8 and 12 objects. The marginal product is `0.95 * 0.92 * 0.88 = 0.76912`, but only 75 objects survive all stages. Conditional success rates are `95/100`, `87/95`, `75/87`; their product is exactly `0.75`. If all stages fail on the same five objects, success is `0.95`, not `0.95^3 = 0.857375`. An independent positive control gives `0.9^2 = 0.81`. The chapter's worked conditional example still gives `0.76912 < 0.95`, hence manual escalation.

The registry now defines conditional errors as well. In `REQ-L3-08-03`, the old condition `нижча за затверджений поріг` became `нижча за затверджений поріг або потрібна оцінка невідома`; the added exact restriction is `Добуток точностей повинен використовувати умовні похибки за правильного префікса; загальні похибки окремих етапів не повинні підміняти їх без обґрунтування незалежності.`

**Reader benefit:** a correct formula is no longer applied to incompatible calibration numbers. **Open:** real conditional calibration data, uncertainty estimates and any error-recovery model were not supplied or measured here. Existing threshold `0.95` was not changed or promoted to a universal policy.
